Our Story

Where Tradition Meets Contemporary Hospitality

Founded with a passion for regional Indian gastronomy, Zaffran & Spice brings centuries-old royal recipes into a welcoming, contemporary dining room. Every curry is slow-simmered from scratch, and every flatbread is baked fresh to order in our charcoal tandoor.

Whether you are celebrating a milestone dinner or sharing casual small plates with friends, our attentive team ensures every visit feels effortless and memorable.

Fresh Ingredients

Whole roasted spices & organic farm produce daily.

Friendly Service

Warm, attentive hospitality tailored to every guest.

Cozy Atmosphere

Candlelit earthen interiors designed for lingering.
